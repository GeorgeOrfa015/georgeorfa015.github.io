Widgets Widgets appear below the search bar and can include any website. Websites with transparent backgrounds work best. All georgeorfa015.github.io subsites will have a transparent background when used for widgets.

Background Images Modify stored images and change the general theme of the random background images. Images are fetched from the Unsplash API every three minutes and the last 20 are cached so there's a new background for every new tab.
If there's a problem with the cache and the images or the page isn't loading correctly, please open the developer console on your browser by either pressing F12, Ctrl+I or right click and Inspect, write clearCache() in the console and press Enter. This will delete your stored images.
